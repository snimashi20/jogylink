# Rollout

A two-person business cannot absorb fifteen modules at once. Sequenced by cash impact: stop the bleeding first.

| Wave | When | Theme | Modules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3 months | Stop the bleeding | F4 field app · I1 batch & expiry · I3 credit ledger · B1 central database |
| 2 | 3–6 months | Connect the shops | F1 retailer ordering · I2 margin engine · I6 CRM · B4 digital payments · B5 dashboard |
| 3 | 6–12 months | Connect producers and consumers | F2 producer portal · F3 consumer storefront · I4 forecasting · I5 returns traceability · B2 procurement · B3 routing |

## Adoption barrier (Technology Acceptance Model, Lecture 2.2)

Many rural shop owners will not install an app, and the single worker may resist being monitored.

- **Perceived usefulness:** show each shop its own credit balance on day one.
- **Perceived ease of use:** WhatsApp is the default channel; the interface is Sinhala-first.
