# Win-win for every stakeholder

The design must bring win-win solutions to all parties, including *internal* customers.

| Stakeholder | What they give | What they gain |
|---|---|---|
| Producers | Answer the owner's reorder call; supply batch and expiry data at dispatch | Forward demand visibility, faster payment, evidence-based quality feedback — **no software to learn** |
| Retail shops | Sign the printed receipt; keep to the payment schedule | Own credit position in writing and by SMS, fresher stock, fewer stockouts, no disputes about what is owed — **no app to install** |
| Supermarket outlets | Shared sell-through data; compliance requirements | Batch traceability, reliable replenishment |
| Individual consumers | Direct orders and reviews | Freshness guarantee, home delivery, festival packs |
| Owner *(internal)* | Investment in the system and discipline to record consistently | Real profit visibility, cash released from dead stock, a business that survives their absence |
| Worker *(internal)* | Learning one POS device | No manual stock counts, a planned route, and **no longer blamed for discrepancies the old system created** — end-of-route reconciliation logs them as data, not accusations |
| Partners | Payment rails, delivery capacity, hosting | Transaction volume, a reference customer |
