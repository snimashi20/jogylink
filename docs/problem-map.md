# Problem map

The twelve problems the owner reported, the modules that answer them, how, and how completely.

| # | Problem | Modules | How | Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Limited working capital | M6 M8 | Credit ledger with ageing and SMS reminders; card payments arrive before dispatch; reports show cash frozen in credit and slow stock | Partly |
| **P2** | Difficult inventory management | M4 M5 | POS deducts stock at every sale; live stock by product and batch, split store / van | **Fully** |
| **P3** | Changing product prices | M1 M2 | Purchase price stored per batch, so true margin is always known; changing wholesale updates the website price automatically | **Fully** |
| **P4** | Cannot calculate real profit | M5 M6 | Purchase cost, both selling prices, returns, wastage and transport are all recorded, so profit computes per product, batch and channel | **Fully** |
| **P5** | Credit sales and collection | M5 M6 | Shop signs a receipt showing the agreed balance and keeps it; the same figure arrives by SMS and sits in the owner's ledger | **Fully** |
| **P6** | Supply and demand problems | M3 | Low stock detected automatically across both channels; sales history lets the owner order before a peak | Partly |
| **P7** | Changes in product quality | M2 M7 | Complaints recorded against a specific batch and supplier, building a quality rating | Partly |
| **P8** | Expiry and weather | M3 M4 | Every batch carries an expiry date; four-stage alerts, FEFO picking, expired stock blocked | **Fully** for tracking |
| **P9** | Product return difficulties | M7 | Photo and uneditable timestamp captured at the shop; claims grouped per supplier and triggered by value or age | **Fully** for evidence |
| **P10** | Introducing new products | M8 | New items appear on the website immediately and on the POS for the van; sell-through data proves an item sells | **Fully** |
| **P11** | Seasonal fluctuations | M3 M8 | Last year's sales by month and festival guide buying; website-only festival packs capture seasonal demand | Partly |
| **P12** | Competition and retention | M5 M8 | Clear records, correct balances, fresher stock — and the website is priced **above** the shop price, so Jogy never competes with its own shops | Partly |

## What technology cannot fix

State these plainly in the report. A solution that claims to fix everything is less credible than
one that is clear about its limits.

- It cannot **give the owner more capital** (P1). It can only return existing money faster.
- It cannot **make a producer hold more stock** (P6) or **change the weather** (P8).
- It cannot **move a distant producer closer** (P9), or force a supplier to accept a claim.
